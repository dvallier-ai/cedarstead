Cedarstead Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Short version. Cedarstead works only on your device. It does not collect, transmit, or store personal data on any external server. The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Who we are

Cedarstead is an iOS and iPadOS photo library cleanup app by Dan Vallier. For questions about this policy, email vallierapps@gmail.com.

How the app uses your photos

Cedarstead accesses your photo library only on this device, to help you review, keep, set aside, and delete photos and videos. It does not upload your library to us or to any other service.

Data we collect

Cedarstead does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any external server. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. Our App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Where your photos and videos stay

Photos and videos stay on your device. If your library uses iCloud Photos, Cedarstead may download items only through Apple’s Photos framework, so you can review them on this device. Those downloads are handled by Apple’s system, not by a Cedarstead server.

Deleting photos and videos

When you choose to delete items, Cedarstead uses Apple’s system confirmation. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where Apple’s usual rules apply.

Children’s privacy

Cedarstead is not directed at children under 13. Because the app does not collect personal data, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact Dan Vallier at vallierapps@gmail.com.
